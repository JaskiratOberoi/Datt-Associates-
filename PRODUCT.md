## Users

Prospective litigants in Delhi and other Indian states — individuals facing matrimonial disputes (divorce, maintenance, custody, 498A), consumers with grievances against companies/builders/insurers, and people entangled in civil or criminal matters. They arrive stressed, often at a legal crisis point, comparing firms, and need to judge credibility fast. Secondary audience: corporate/institutional clients and referring professionals evaluating the firm's pedigree.

## Product Purpose

Informational website for Datt & Associates, a Delhi-based firm of advocates, solicitors and consultants. Success = a visitor understands the firm's practice areas and partner credentials within seconds and initiates contact (call/visit/enquiry) for a consultation.

## Positioning

A firm that pairs 40+ years of corporate finance-and-management depth (the managing partner's pre-practice career) with full-spectrum litigation reach — from district courts up to the Supreme Court of India, and across the entire consumer forum hierarchy (District, State, National Commission). Specialists in matrimonial and consumer dispute cases, which most general litigation firms treat as sidelines.

## Operating Context

Indian litigation practice: appearances at all Delhi district courts, the Delhi High Court, the Supreme Court of India, and District/State/National Consumer Disputes Redressal Commissions. Clients typically first contact by phone or walk-in; the website is the credibility check before that call.

## Evidence on Hand

Only the facts in the brief (partner credentials, practice scope, court reach). No testimonials, case results, client names, photos, or press exist on hand — none may be fabricated as real claims. Illustrative/synthetic material must be labeled.

## Product Principles

1. Credibility is carried by verifiable specifics — degrees, years, courts, forums — never by superlatives or invented proof.
2. The distressed litigant is the first reader: clarity and reassurance outrank cleverness.
3. Every page must survive BCI scrutiny: inform, never solicit.
4. Contact is the conversion: the path to a phone call or visit stays visible everywhere.
5. Placeholders are honest — clearly marked, easy for the firm to replace.
