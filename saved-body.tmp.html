Manner of Proceeding

How a matter moves through the firm, from first meeting to final order.

Clause the First. Consultation & Opinion

The matter is heard in full at chambers. The firm gives a candid written opinion — including, where it is the honest answer, that litigation is not worth its cost — and settles the strategy and fee before anything is filed.

Clause the Second. Pleadings & Filing

Plaint, petition, complaint or reply is drafted by the partners themselves, settled with the client line by line, and filed in the proper forum with every annexure in order.

Clause the Third. Appearances & Hearings

A partner appears at every effective hearing. After each date the client receives a plain-language note of what happened and what comes next.

Clause the Fourth. Resolution

Decree, order, settlement or award — and where the outcome warrants it, appeal to the next forum. The file closes only when the client's remedy is actually in hand.
