# Surface brief — index.html (firm site, single page)

Scope: the entire public site of Datt Associates, one static page. Visitor mode: Persuade.

Audience & job: distressed litigants (matrimonial, consumer, civil, criminal) in Delhi/NCR plus referring professionals; job = judge credibility fast and call/visit the firm. Action: "Request a Consultation" → contact block (placeholder phone/address rendered as deed blanks).

Proof: verifiable specifics only — partner degrees, years, court/forum reach. No testimonials, results, or superlatives (BCI compliance). BCI disclaimer gate on entry, styled as an affidavit declaration with stamp-to-accept.

Chosen direction (seed 9ecdd516, own grounded candidate #4, user-approved): **Stamp Paper Engraving** — the site as an engraved Indian legal instrument. Guilloche security borders drawn as working SVG, buff security-paper ground, committed intaglio green fields, rubber-stamp red endorsements, court-fee gold, serial numbers, microprint rules, perforated stamp frames, deed-blank placeholders.

Sequence: hero stamp → CTA endorsement strip → Partners (full stamp cards) → Schedule of Practice → Forums → Procedure → Contact. Comps rendered from real code (image generation unavailable; substitution user-approved), so comp colors/type are the shipped tokens exactly.

Not to be literalized: comp partner cards were condensed previews — the built section keeps full bios/portraits; comp pages held only 3 schedule rows — all five ship.

Memorable moment: the disclaimer gate — accepting slams a red "AGREED" rubber stamp and the instrument unfolds. Secondary signature: the postmark cancelling the hero stamp.

Unresolved: real address/phone/email, partner photos, enrolment numbers, firm founding year — shipped as marked blanks.

Gold accent placement (cited per finish review): court-fee gold lives on the paper sheets — the SPECIALISATION fee marks in the schedule, the quals rules inside the partner stamps, and the tint-sheet borders. It is deliberately absent from the green hero stamp, where paper-on-green engraving and the red postmark/serial carry the accents. This is placement, not removal.
